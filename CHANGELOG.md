# What's new in Clawd Slayer

The changes you'd notice in the Clawd Slayer universe, newest first. The full, detailed history (every fix, every version) lives in [Clawd Clash](https://github.com/rodclemen/clawd-clash).

## Clawd Clash 4.0.0 (2026-10-23)

- **Every name says "clawd".** The puns that only had "claw" get their "d": Buffy, Dawn and Joyce Clawdmers, Willow Rosenclawd, Tara Maclawd, Jenny Clawdlendar, Kate Lockclawd, Miss Kitty Fanclawdstico, Mayor Wilclawds and Glorificlawds. Draclawla is Clawdula now.
- **The Welcome to Sunnydale sign** is drawn in pixels, like the rest of the town.

## Clawd Clash 3.7.0 (2026-10-18)

- **A new universe.** Clawd Slayer joins Star Clawds, Marvelous Clawds, Detective Clawds, Doctor Clawd, The Clawd Files, Stevie Clawdberg and Clawd of the Rings: pick it in **Settings → Universe**, or in the screen saver's Options.
- **83 characters from Buffy the Vampire Slayer and Angel**, each with a signature move. Buffy Clawdmers flipping the stake and catching it without looking, Willow Rosenclawd's floating pencil (*"Resolve face!"*), Xander's rocket launcher, Rupert Gyles polishing the glasses, Spyke's chip zapping mid-punch (*"Bloody hell!"*), the Slayers and the Potentials, and Angel Investigations, Angstel turning into a felt puppet for a second. Facing them, the Old Master pressed against the invisible barrier, Drusillah and Miss Edith, the Mayor's wet wipes, Glorificlawds flickering into Ben, the First Evil, Dark Willow (*"Bored now."*), the Gentlemen, Olaff's hammer and Wolfram & Hart. HEM turns up in a Watcher's tweed jacket with a wooden stake.
- **20 places**, from the Sunnydale High Library and the Bronze to Restfield Cemetery, the Magic Box, Spyke's crypt, the Hellmouth, Glory's tower, the Doublemeat Palace, the Hyperion Hotel, Caritas and Wolfram & Hart, each with something moving in it.
- **Rides:** the yellow school bus picks up the Scooby Gang, the Slayers and the Potentials, Spyke's painted-out DeSoto the vampires, Angstel's convertible Angel Investigations; everyone else walks.
- **Tabula Rasa.** When one of that episode's eight compacts its context, Willow's forgetting spell goes off: a crystal glows, they don't know who they are (*"JOAN?"*), then it cracks and it all comes back.
- **Hush**: with the Gentlemen on stage, every speech bubble comes up empty. **The full moon**: on nights the real moon is full, Ozz and Veruka stay wolves.
- **The First Evil** now and then becomes one of the Big Bads before it for a few seconds: the Old Master, the Mayor, Glorificlawds or Adamm.
- **Thirteen transitions** from the show: the opening titles, Dusting, the Watcher's tome, Willow's spell, Bored Now, the Hellmouth, Hush, Once More With Feeling, sunrise, the Key's portal, the coat swish, the lift at Wolfram & Hart and Restless.
- **Arch-enemies face off first** (Buffy and the Old Master, Willow and Glorificlawds, Xander and Dark Willow, Giles and Ethan Raine, Spyke and Clawdula…), and Angstel and Spyke never lead two sessions back to back.
- **Clawd Royale**: Clawd Slayer's cast joins everyone else's in one scene when you pick Clawd Royale.
- **A trailer of its own**, at the top of this page.
